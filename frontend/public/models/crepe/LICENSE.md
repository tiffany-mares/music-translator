# CREPE model — attribution

The files in this directory (model.json + weight shards) are the
TensorFlow.js build of the CREPE pitch-tracking model from
https://github.com/marl/crepe (fetched from the official web demo at
https://marl.github.io/crepe/model/), MIT License, Copyright (c) 2018
Music and Audio Research Laboratory (MARL), New York University. Vendored
so LyraLearn serves it first-party (no third-party runtime dependency,
no CORS exposure).

CREPE: A Convolutional Representation for Pitch Estimation —
Jong Wook Kim, Justin Salamon, Peter Li, Juan Pablo Bello (ICASSP 2018).
